2. ARCHIVED ORDERS
PASS: Archive preserves order items; restoring returns customer visibility. Archive/restore itself does not change stock.
HIGH: Soft-deleting an order removes it from customer history and makes its customer invoice inaccessible. This is the current behavior; confirm the intended archive policy when repairing it.
HIGH: Archived Stripe orders cannot be found by the webhook lookup. A payment that completes after archiving cannot be finalized through that lookup.
HIGH: Archiving a paid coupon order bypasses the checkout per-customer usage check. Reproduced using a freshly loaded coupon with global limit 10 and per-customer limit 1. Before archive the second use is rejected; after archive it is accepted. Redemption history still exists, so the later redemption service can disagree with checkout.
Recommendation: Separate admin archive visibility from the durable customer/payment order record; consistently account for archived purchases in coupon limits.
