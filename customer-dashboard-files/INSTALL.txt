INSTALLATION
1. Back up C:\xampp\htdocs\ArizonaOutfits and your database.
2. Stop php artisan serve while replacing files.
3. Use FILES.html for every download link and exact destination. You can open it
   in your browser from this folder.
4. For each numbered .txt file, copy ALL its contents into the mapped destination.
   Create any missing folder or file. Save the destination with its real .php or
   .blade.php name, not .txt.
5. Install all 92 files together for the complete synchronized snapshot. This set
   already includes the previous admin, customer-security and bell replacements.
   Do not install older packages over it afterward.
6. Keep your existing migrations/database. No new migration is supplied here.
7. Open a terminal in C:\xampp\htdocs\ArizonaOutfits and run:
C:\xampp\php\php.exe artisan optimize:clear
C:\xampp\php\php.exe artisan serve
8. Refresh with Ctrl+F5. Sign in as a customer and open /account.
   If your dashboard URL differs, use the My Account link on the storefront.

CUSTOMER TESTS AFTER INSTALLATION
A. Overview: compare order count and paid spending with admin Customers. Each
   currency must stay separate. Out-for-delivery belongs in transit.
B. My Orders: try order-number/tracking search, order status and payment status.
   Click the overview awaiting-full-payment link and compare its count.
C. Open a current and archived order. Check payment/delivery data against admin.
D. Open View Invoice and Download PDF. Confirm order currency and totals.
E. Admin-only notes must stay hidden; customer-visible notes must appear.
F. Change order/payment/tracking in admin, refresh the customer detail and check
   the new status. Refresh admin to check its bell notification.
G. Test Login & Security, profile updates and favorites from the new links.
H. Verify an email change: the new address logs in, the old one stops logging in.
I. Change password through both forms. Both reject the current/previous password.
J. A customer with current/archived orders cannot self-delete. A disposable
   customer without orders can delete; a coexisting admin session stays logged in.
K. Block a customer in admin. Their next customer request must be denied.
L. Try another customer order URL: detail and invoices must refuse access.
