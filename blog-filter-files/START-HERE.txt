- Try Newest first, Oldest first, Title A-Z, and Title Z-A.
- Combine search and category to narrow the results.
- Search for a word that does not exist; expect No articles found.
- Use View all articles to reset from the empty state.
- Check the page on a phone or narrow browser window.

WHAT THIS ADDS
Keyword search across titles, slugs, summaries, stored article content,
category names, and author names. Multiple search words must all match.
Category filtering also matches the primary category.
Category options show their parent hierarchy. Subcategories are optional.
Author and inclusive publication date filters can be combined.
Result counts, removable filter chips, and clear/reset links are included.
Only published articles appear; draft-only categories/authors are excluded.
Pagination keeps the selected filters and shows 12 articles per page.
With only three matching articles, pagination will not appear.

Search uses text stored in the database. It does not read the body text
inside manually written article Blade templates. Publication dates refer
to published_at, not the date the article was first created.

No additional routes, database migrations, CSS files, or JavaScript files
are required. The view includes the new section's styles.
Existing single-article handling remains in the replacement controller.
